# NIFTY 50 Stock Market Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An interactive Excel dashboard analysing the NIFTY 50 companies across five major sectors: Financial Services, Information Technology, Energy, Consumer Goods, and Metals. The project explores stock market trends, sector-wise contributions, and market capitalisation to understand the distribution and performance of India's leading publicly listed companies.

## Objectives
- Analyse stock market trends and company performance.
- Compare market capitalisation across different sectors.
- Identify sector-wise contributions to the NIFTY 50.
- Examine stock price movements and differences in company performance.
- Present key financial indicators through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Techniques
- **Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and organisation
- Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts
- KPI Cards
- Interactive Slicers
- Timeline Filters
- Data Visualisation

## Key Analysis & Insights
- Financial Services accounted for approximately 26.68% of the analysed market capitalisation, followed by IT at 23.53% and Energy at 17.32%.
- The five leading companies across the selected sectors collectively represented 34.16% of the total market capitalisation.
- Stock price movements varied considerably across companies, highlighting differences in market performance.
- The analysis demonstrated the importance of sector composition when evaluating the broader market.

## Dashboard Features
- Interactive filtering using slicers and timelines.
- KPI cards for quick access to key metrics.
- Sector-wise market capitalisation comparisons.
- Visual representation of stock price trends.
- Company-level performance analysis.

## Future Scope
The project can be extended by incorporating annual returns, CAGR, stock volatility, fundamental financial indicators, and historical sector concentration. Predictive analysis could also be introduced to explore potential future market trends.
